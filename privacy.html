Plumb & Level Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · Updated 28 October 2026

Plumb & Level is developed by Daniel Maldonado as an individual. Local campaign play works offline without an app account. The developer operates no advertising, tracking or analytics service.

On your device

The app saves progress, stars, earned cosmetics, settings and unfinished attempts locally. Optional personalized help learns from placement outcomes on this device; its observations and practice suggestions are not uploaded. You can turn this help off or reset it in Settings. There is no cloud AI service or downloaded learning model.

Apple services

When available, iCloud key-value storage synchronizes campaign progress through your Apple Account. Unfinished attempts remain local. The developer cannot access your private iCloud progress. Game Center is optional; connecting uses your Apple Game Center identity and submits game scores and achievement progress to Apple when configured. Apple controls account privacy and leaderboard visibility. Apple processes the app purchase; the developer receives no payment-card details or Apple Account password.

Stickers and icons

Sharing a sticker opens the system sharing interface. It is sent only when you choose a recipient and send it. Home Screen icon choices are handled on your device and are not synchronized as gameplay progress.

Diagnostics and support

Apple may provide App Store/TestFlight diagnostics according to your Apple settings. If you contact support, the information you voluntarily send is used to respond. Do not include passwords or financial information. Apple services follow Apple's privacy policy.

Deleting progress

Settings → Restart Game clears campaign progress after confirmation and synchronizes that reset when iCloud is available. It does not remove Game Center records or revoke a purchase. Reset Learned Help clears the local learning observations. Deleting the app removes local data subject to Apple backup/device settings. Contact support about information you supplied by email.

Privacy Policy

Sort & Settle 1.5 · Updated 20 October 2026

Local gameplay works offline. The developer does not operate an account service, advertising system or analytics service.

Local progress and settings

The app stores your level, board, undo history, progress and comfort settings on your device. The paid-download edition includes every level and works offline. Earlier editions may cache a Full Version in-app purchase entitlement for offline access.

Optional Apple services

If iCloud is available, progress can synchronize through Apple's iCloud key-value service under your Apple Account. Conflicting boards offer a choice in the app. The developer does not receive access to your private iCloud progress.

In version 1.5, Game Center is optional and available from Settings. Choosing Connect shares completed-level scores with Apple and allows the app to show Game Center friends' display names, ranks and scores. Scores are stored by Apple; a friends-only display in the app does not make Apple's leaderboard database private. Apple manages Game Center account information and privacy settings. The developer does not receive your account password.

Apple handles the paid app download. The paid-download edition has no in-app purchases; earlier editions use Apple's StoreKit service for Full Version purchases and restoration. Apple handles payment information. The developer does not receive your payment card information or Apple Account credentials.

These services require connectivity. Local puzzles, undo, hints, themes and comfort settings do not require an account or internet connection. There are no third-party advertising, tracking or analytics SDKs in the app.

Feedback and diagnostics

On devices with an email app, Send feedback can open a draft containing app version, level and device/OS information. Apple TV shows contact details so you can email from another device. Nothing is sent until you choose to send the email. Information you voluntarily send to the support email address is used to respond to your request.

Apple may provide App Store and TestFlight diagnostics according to your Apple privacy settings. Apple services are governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Deleting progress

Settings → Start over resets saved progress. If iCloud sync is available, the reset also synchronizes to your other devices. This does not erase scores already submitted to Game Center or revoke an Apple purchase. Game Center account and score controls are managed through Apple.

Deleting the app removes its local app data, subject to Apple backups and device settings. Contact support about information you voluntarily provided by email.

Children

The game has no ads, chat or user-posted content. Game Center is optional. Game Center, the App Store download purchase and legacy in-app purchases are subject to Apple Account, family and parental controls. Settings includes support contact details, this policy and a link to the App Store.

Changes

This policy describes version 1.5. Earlier versions do not include Game Center. Material changes will be reflected in the policy date.
